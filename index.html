Vote Math

The room is split, the bylaws say a percent, and someone has to count to the next whole person.

Boundary: counting only Vote Math counts from the percents and tallies you type. It does not say what your bylaws require, whether a vote is valid, or how a meeting should be run. Your own rules decide that.

The quorum truth

40 members, quorum 25%: 10 must be present. 12 present: over by 2. Always rounded up to a whole person.

The threshold truth

For 14, against 9, abstain 3, at 60%: of the 23 votes for and against, 14 are needed, exactly enough. Of the 26 votes cast, 16 are needed, short by 2. Both bases, side by side.

The margin truth

14 for, 9 against: for leads by 5. 3 votes changing sides would reverse it.
